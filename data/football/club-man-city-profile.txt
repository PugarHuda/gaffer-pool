Club: Manchester City

Date: 2026-06-01

Style of play: Positional possession (juego de posición). Builds with a back
three in possession by inverting a fullback into midfield, creating a 3-2-5
attacking shape. Aims to fix defenders with width from the wingers and attack
the half-spaces with the "10" and an inverted runner. Immediate counter-press
(gegenpress) on any turnover in the opposition half.

Strengths:
- Controls tempo and territory; sustained pressure wears down low blocks.
- Rodri (or his replacement) as single pivot dictates rhythm and screens counters.
- Rest-defence structure (2-3 behind the ball) limits transitions conceded.

Weaknesses:
- If the single pivot is neutralised or injured, build-up quality drops sharply.
- High line can be exploited by direct balls in behind before the press sets.
- Can struggle for a "plan B" when a deep block concedes zero space centrally.

Key players: Rodri (single pivot / deep-lying playmaker), Erling Haaland
(centre-forward / penalty-box finisher), inverted fullbacks.
