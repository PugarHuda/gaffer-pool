Match report: Real Madrid 2 - 2 Manchester City
Competition: UEFA Champions League, Round of 16 (1st leg)
Date: 2026-03-05
Venue: Santiago Bernabeu

Summary: An even, high-quality tie. City controlled possession (61%) but Madrid
were more efficient in transition. Both Madrid goals came within 8 seconds of
winning the ball back — exactly the transition threat flagged in the club
profile. City's goals came from sustained pressure and a set-piece.

Key numbers:
Possession: Madrid 39% / City 61%
Shots: Madrid 11 / City 16
xG: Madrid 1.9 / City 2.1
Big chances: Madrid 3 / City 3
Goals: Bellingham 2 (Madrid); Haaland 1, Rodri 1 (City)

Tactical takeaways:
- Madrid deliberately ceded the ball and attacked the space behind City's high
  line on the counter. Worked twice.
- City struggled to contain Bellingham's late runs; Rodri was repeatedly pulled
  out of his screening zone, opening the counter lanes.
- The tie is finely balanced going into the 2nd leg. To progress, Madrid must
  keep transition discipline; City must occupy Rodri's cover better or protect
  the space behind the line.
